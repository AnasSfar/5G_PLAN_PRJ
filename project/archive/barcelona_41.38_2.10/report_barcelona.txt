C-RAN DESIGN: BBU-POOL AND RRH PLACEMENT - REPORT (DRAFT, GROWS EVERY WEEK)
==========================================================================
Final report = the weekly sections below + last simulations, analysis and conclusions.


1. SCENARIO AND DATA (week 1)
-----------------------------
1.1 Study area
    Barcelona metropolitan area, centre 41.38 N / 2.10 E, box of 30 x 30 km
    (1.920-2.280 E, 41.245-41.515 N). It contains the whole dense city, Badalona, Sant Cugat and
    the Llobregat delta, and also sparse zones (hills, sea), so RRH density varies a lot.
    A first box centred at 2.00 E was discarded after a map check showed it cut the city in half.

1.2 Data source
    Infoantenas (Ministerio para la Transformacion Digital), official register of mobile base
    stations in Spain. Its map loads a GeoJSON service per bounding box; the area was split into
    15 x 15 tiles of 2 x 2 km (225 requests, 0.5 s apart) and every raw answer was stored.
    The GeoJSON contains site code, operator, address and coordinates, but not the technology.
    The technology was inferred from the frequency bands listed on each site's detail page
    (4G: 700/800/1800/2600 MHz; 5G: 3.5 GHz and 26 GHz only; 900 and 2100 MHz ignored because
    they are shared with 2G/3G; 700 MHz can carry 4G or 5G so it counts as 4G only).

1.3 Processing
    Remove duplicates -> read bands -> label 4G / 5G / 4G+5G -> drop sites without 4G/5G ->
    merge towers closer than 50 m (all operators) into one RRH keeping the operator list ->
    master JSON and CSV -> Folium map.

1.4 Results
    Raw: 3,254 features in 225 tiles = 3,253 distinct sites.
    3,253 distinct sites -> 2,779 with a 4G or 5G band (474 dropped: only 2G/3G bands) ->
    1,437 RRHs after merging towers within 50 m (at most 6 towers in one RRH).
    Labels: 756 RRHs 4G+5G, 680 RRHs 4G only, 1 RRH 5G only (757 RRHs carry 5G, always via a
    3.5/26 GHz band). Operators present (RRHs): Movistar 904, Vodafone 722, Orange 717, DS Mobile
    Networks 71 (70 of them merged with another operator on the same tower).
    Spot-check with OpenCelliD (3 areas of ~1 km2, data/spotcheck_opencellid.csv): every RRH of ours
    has an OpenCelliD LTE/NR cell within 500 m (26/26, 3/3, 3/3). Supporting evidence only: small
    sample, lenient tolerance, and it cannot show missing sites. Infoantenas remains the reference.

1.5 Limitations
    - Technology is inferred from bands, not declared by the source.
    - 5G needs a 3.5/26 GHz band (strict rule); a band assigned is not a carrier on air.
    - Cross-check with OpenCelliD is small (3 areas).
