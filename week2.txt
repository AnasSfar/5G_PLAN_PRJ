WEEK 2 - PHASE 1: MINIMISE DELAY
================================
Goal (professor's review "Project review phase 1 - min delay", thesis section 3.3.3): assign each
RRH to the closest BBU pool so that the round-trip fronthaul delay stays below 200 us; give for each
RRH the list of possible pools and the optimal one; mark too-far RRHs as unserved; no capacity limit.
Then decide how many pools are needed and which ones are not relevant.

2. DECISIONS AND REASONS
------------------------
- RRHs: the review asks for "around 200" RRHs and 19 pools (as in the thesis, 200 sites / 19 pools in
  Porto). We draw 200 of the 2,085 real core RRHs at random (seed 42). A simple random sample keeps the
  real density (dense centre, sparse edges), so the scenario stays realistic. N_RRH_REAL = None runs
  the full 2,085 (done in step 10 as a check).
- Synthetic RRHs: N_SYNTHETIC (variable), uniform in the 30 x 30 km box, at least 200 m from any other
  RRH, id SYN001..., is_synthetic = true, drawn as triangles. Reference run: 0. Step 10 adds 50 to 400.
- Traffic pattern, two modes (TRAFFIC_MODE):
    random: exact shares from TRAFFIC_SHARES (input; default 50 % mixed, 30 % office, 20 % residential);
    geo (reference): office if inside a known business district (Castellana axis Colon - Plaza de
    Castilla, AZCA, Cuatro Torres, Distrito Telefonica, Arroyo de la Vega, IFEMA / Campo de las
    Naciones, Julian Camarillo, Mendez Alvaro, Ciudad Universitaria, Ciudad de la Imagen); mixed if in
    the historic centre (Sol / Gran Via) or at the airport, or if the site is isolated (<= 4 real core
    RRHs within 1 km: large cells covering several land uses, as the thesis observes for its outer
    cells); residential otherwise. Result: 120 residential, 30 office, 50 mixed (60 / 15 / 25 %).
    Why a rule: OpenStreetMap land-use data could not be downloaded from our environment, and the
    operators' traffic is not public. The zone centres are approximate and drawn on fig02.
- Delay -> distance, thesis eq. (3.3): d_max = v * RTT / 2 with v = 2e8 m/s (200 km/ms, thesis
  section 4.1). RTT = 200 us -> d_max = 20 km. Straight lines, as in the thesis; ROUTE_FACTOR in
  config.py can lengthen the fibre if wanted (1.5 would give 13.3 km).
- Candidate pools: 25 (review: "start with a large number, 25 for example, and fix the positions").
  Every real RRH is a candidate site; farthest-first k-center starts at the RRH nearest the centre
  (Bernabeu) and then always takes the RRH farthest from the pools already chosen. The positions are
  computed once and saved, so adding synthetic RRHs never moves them. Ids P01..P25 follow that order.
- Minimise Delay algorithm (thesis fig. 3.5, without capacity): distance to every pool -> list of
  possible pools (d <= d_max) -> 0: unserved; 1: that pool; > 1: the nearest.
- Reducing the number of pools: backward elimination. Start with the 25 pools and remove, one at a
  time, the pool whose removal increases the mean RTT the least, never leaving an extra RRH unserved.
  The removal order ranks the pools from least to most relevant. We also evaluate the farthest-first
  prefixes (first K candidates = k-center solution for K) for comparison.
- Choice of K (K_ACTIVE = "auto"): the smallest K whose mean RTT is within 10 % (KNEE_TOL) of the
  25-pool value. A pure delay criterion would give K = 1 (see results), which is useless for the load
  balancing of phase 2, so we keep the pools that still bring a visible delay reduction.
- Three categories per pool (review 3c): 1 = RRHs that can only reach this pool; 2 = RRHs for which
  it is the nearest pool but which have alternatives; 3 = RRHs that can reach it but have a nearer one.


3. RESULTS (reference: 200 real RRHs, geo traffic, RTT <= 200 us, d_max = 20 km)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 25 pools: 0 unserved, mean RTT 18.2 us (std 10.2), max 37.8 us, mean distance 1.82 km.
- Removal order (least relevant first): P25 P11 P10 P07 P06 P24 | P04 P02 P05 P22 P03 P17 P08 P20 P13
  P09 P12 P18 P14 P19 P21 P15 P16 P23 (P01 is the last pool standing).
- Selected K = 19 (P25, P11, P10, P07, P06, P24 switched off): 0 unserved, mean RTT 19.9 us (std 11.2),
  max 68.2 us, mean distance 1.99 km. Same number of pools as the thesis and the review.
- Mean RTT vs K (backward elimination): K=1 71.3 us, 4 43.3, 8 29.7, 12 24.5, 16 21.5, 19 19.9, 25 18.2.
  Farthest-first prefixes are worse at intermediate K (K=8: 43.7 us) because k-center first places
  pools at the edges, where there are few RRHs; it minimises the maximum, not the mean.
- Smallest K that serves all 200 RRHs: 1 at 200 us (one pool at the centre, max RTT 189 us),
  2 at 150 us, 6 at 100 us, 17 at 50 us. With 200 us the delay is NOT the binding constraint in a
  30 x 30 km area: only the box corners (21.2 km from the centre) are beyond 20 km of a central pool.
- Options per RRH with K = 19 at 200 us: 10 to 19 reachable pools (mean 16.8); no RRH has a single
  option, so category 1 is empty for every pool. Category 2 (assigned): P19 33, P01 32, P16 18, P15 16,
  P13 14, P23 14, P18 12, P09 10, P20 / P21 / P22 8, P14 6, P08 / P17 4, P03 / P05 / P12 3, P02 / P04 2.
  The edge pools P02, P03, P04, P05, P12 serve only 2-3 RRHs each.
- Strict budget 50 us (d_max 5 km) with the same 19 pools: 1 RRH unserved (RRH1729 in the west,
  6.8 km from P15; it was covered by P06, switched off), 50 RRHs with a single option, mean 2.35
  options. Exclusive RRHs sit around the peripheral pools (P15 9, P18 6, P21 6, P23 5); the central
  pools P01, P19, P13, P09 have none.
- Robustness (same 19 pools): +50 / +100 / +200 / +400 synthetic RRHs -> 0 unserved at 200 us, mean RTT
  23.5 / 25.7 / 29.0 / 30.6 us; at 50 us 12 / 20 / 40 / 68 unserved, almost all synthetic (uniform sites
  fall where the real network, and therefore the pools, are sparse). All 2,085 real RRHs -> 0 unserved,
  mean 21.7 us, max 99.9 us; 33 unserved at 50 us. Every invariant check passed.
- Comparison with the thesis (Porto, 19 pools, 150 us): mean 23.1 us, std 17.6 us (table 4.4); our
  19.9 / 11.2 us is of the same order. Its 1-pool case is about 55 us mean with 24 RRHs unserved
  (fig. 4.17); ours is 71 us with none unserved, because our limit is 200 us instead of 150 us.
